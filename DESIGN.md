# DESIGN: Juan Miguel, signal path

## Brief

**Subject:** Juan Miguel, computer engineer and technology consultant in Madrid.
**Audience:** Potential clients, technical leads and peers who need to judge whether he can
understand a system and carry a decision through implementation.
**Job:** Make the value legible in the first frame, give proof through two real engineering
projects, and open a direct conversation.
**Language:** Spanish. The repository, the existing content and the location are Spanish; the
owner wrote the request in English. Every visible string stays in Spanish.
**Assets:** `apps/web/src/assets/profile.webp` (owner supplied), the repository itself.
**Constraints:** Keep the Astro + pnpm + Turborepo + TypeScript monorepo, the content
collections, the component package split and the deployment workflow already in the repo.
Keep everything on one page: discipline (method and capabilities), work, experience, profile
and contact. Project detail opens as a sheet, not as a separate route. Use Tabler for interface
icons and Simple Icons for technology marks.

### Assumptions (replace with real facts when available)

- Madrid is the working location (carried over from the previous `home.json` metadata).
- The email and GitHub profile in the previous content are the public contact details.
- Accenture technology consulting ran from 2024 to 2025.
- PwC consulting since 2025 (Business Security Solutions division).
- The two projects are the two real engineering outcomes visible in this repository: the
  content-driven portfolio and the TFG automation of academic exercise correction. No client
  names, awards, metrics or testimonials are invented.
- "Available for new projects" is inferred from the existing badge. Remove it if it is not true.
- The supplied portrait is Juan Miguel.

## Concept

```
Idea:        The portfolio is a signal path. One trace connects profile, work,
             experience and contact like nodes in the same system.
Metaphor:    A circuit schematic: a bus, branches, ports and a pulse.
Signature:   A scroll-driven trace in the page margin. It draws as the visitor
             moves, branches into every section node, and carries a pulse that
             keeps breathing when the page is still.
Emotion:     Calm precision. The sense of a person who can see the whole system
             and knows where the signal goes.
Motion verb: Route. Signals travel, branch and lock at a node.
Screenshot:  Giant stepped name, a red node beside it, a hairline trace entering
             from the margin, and mono coordinates in the corners.
```

The concept survives the whole site: section labels are nodes, project rows are ports, the
project sheets are branches that open from a port, the dark contact field is the endpoint and
the 404 is a disconnected endpoint. Everything runs on one page, so the trace never restarts.

### Three concepts considered

| | A. Signal path | B. Technical datasheet | C. Noise becomes signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Territory | Material of the profession | Formal system | Process metaphor |
| One sentence | The site is one circuit connecting the person's work. | The site is a specification sheet for a person: fields, values, tolerances. | The site starts as noise and resolves into legible work. |
| Signature | Scroll-driven bus with a live pulse and branches | Red annotation pen completes the sheet | A field of static resolves as you scroll |
| Risk | Could become decorative circuitry | A layout, not a behaviour; broadsheet default | Generic AI/tech trope; shader-heavy and expensive |
| Scores (O/F/Fe/P) | 4 / 5 / 5 / 2 | 3 / 3 / 5 / 4 | 4 / 3 / 3 / 3 |

**Pick A.** It is generated by the actual work, it gives the site a behaviour rather than a
skin, and it is cheap enough to execute precisely without WebGL. The technical-datasheet
restraint is kept as a detail: indices, coordinates, rules and tabular figures.

## Art direction

### Palette

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `#F2F1EC` | Paper. The whole site except the closing field. |
| Ink | `#141413` | Text, rules, the closing field. |
| Muted | `#62615B` | Secondary text. 5.49:1 on ground. |
| Line | `rgb(20 20 19 / 14%)` | Hairlines, grid, inactive trace. |
| Accent | `#D93A2B` | The signal: nodes, live trace, large type, focus. |
| Field | `#141413` | The closing contact/footer field and the sheet scrim. |

Small accent text uses `#B72C20` on paper (5.47:1); on ink it uses `#FF7A6B` (7.24:1).
`#D93A2B` is reserved for graphics and large text where the 3:1 large-text threshold applies.

No gradient identity, no glass, no soft shadows, no rounded pills.

### Type

- **Display and text:** Switzer (Fontshare, self-hosted). A neutral Swiss grotesk with a
  tighter, more contemporary voice than Inter.
- **Data:** JetBrains Mono 400/500, used only for indices, coordinates, status, years and
  technology names. Not every label.
- Body 17 to 18 px, 1.55 leading, measure 38 to 62 characters.
- Display tracking -0.045em, leading 0.84 to 0.9. The name is composed as two stepped lines,
  not a centered hero.
- Numbers use tabular figures. Real quotes and Spanish accents are kept.

### Grid and rhythm

- 12 columns, shell `min(100% - 2 * gutter, 92rem)`, gutter `clamp(1rem, 3vw, 2.5rem)`.
- Strong left edge: node labels in column 1, content from column 4 or 5, metadata in columns
  10 to 12. The trace runs in the outer margin, just left of the shell.
- Section rhythm is deliberately varied: full-viewport hero, airy work index, tight
  capabilities table, medium experience block, airy profile, full-bleed dark contact.
- One radius rule: 0. One shadow rule: none. One border rule: 1 px.

### Motion language

- One clock: GSAP ticker drives Lenis, ScrollTrigger and the trace. No independent rAF loops.
- `expo.out` for entrances, `expo.inOut` for sheets, `power3.out` for hover feedback.
- Hover feedback 250 to 350 ms. Text reveals 0.9 to 1.2 s, stagger 0.04 to 0.08.
- Project sheets rise from the bottom edge in 0.72 s and leave in 0.5 s. The native dialog owns
  focus containment, Escape and the top layer; the page behind is locked, never swapped.
- Loader: 0.6 s floor on touch and 0.8 s on desktop, capped at 1.5 s, about 2.4 s including the
  exit. Counter plus a red bus line; exits into the hero.
- Primary targets answer in the motion verb: project rows invert and a branch reaches them;
  buttons flood from the left; arrows travel. Nav uses an underline draw. Utility links may
  only change colour.
- The trace has an idle state: the pulse at the current scroll position breathes slowly. It
  pauses off screen and is static under reduced motion.
- Under reduced motion: no Lenis, no reveals, no loader theatrics, no pulse, and sheets open
  and close immediately without the rise. The trace is drawn complete and all content is
  visible.

### The genericness test

The reflex answer to "Swiss minimal portfolio for an engineer" is a centered hero, a neon
green accent on near-black, three rounded feature cards, an uppercase eyebrow above every
section and a grid of fake dashboards. This site deliberately rejects all five. The Swiss
restraint comes from the grid, type and one red signal, not from a template. The numbered
labels are not decoration: they are the order of nodes on the bus, and they are useful for
orientation. The project visuals are diagrams, not invented screenshots.

## Content rules

- Spanish, sentence case, no em or en dashes in visible text.
- Specific verbs and nouns. No "elevate", "seamless", "passionate", "cutting-edge".
- No invented clients, logos, awards or metrics.
- The home and the 404 have unique titles and descriptions. Both are written in the same voice.

## Technical decisions

- Keep the monorepo: `apps/web`, `packages/brand`, `packages/ui`, shared TypeScript config.
- Keep Astro content collections and Zod validation. Add page fields and project metadata to
  the existing schemas instead of moving content into components.
- Add GSAP and Lenis, the engine lifecycle in `apps/web/src/engine`, and a small Astro adapter
  that boots once. There is no ClientRouter and no client-side page swap to keep in sync.
- One page carries profile, approach, work, capabilities, experience and contact. The two
  project sheets are native `<dialog>` elements mounted at body level, outside `main`, so no
  stacking context can trap the overlay; `#proyecto-slug` is the no-JS target fallback. The
  404 is the only other route.
- Add `astro-icon` with Tabler (interface) and Simple Icons (technology marks). Only used icons
  ship.
- Self-host Switzer and JetBrains Mono. Preload only the two fonts used above the fold.
- One committed light theme. The final contact and footer are the dark field. No theme toggle:
  it would double the design surface without helping the brief.
- No WebGL. The signature is DOM/SVG and stays at 60 fps on a phone.
- Project plates are hand-authored inline SVG in `ProjectPlate.astro`, not screenshots or
  generated images. Both variants share one 960x560 coordinate system, an 80 px grid, boxes,
  ports and a labelled bus; below 900 px the same steps render as an accessible ordered list.
- Keep the first load small: Astro inlines the CSS into the document, only the intro is split
  up front, and below-the-fold reveals and SplitText runs are built when they approach the
  viewport. The trace caches its document offset and batches reads before writes; pulses,
  underlines and hover states animate transform and opacity, not layout or paint properties.
- Portraits use responsive `srcset` widths, so a 1x screen does not download the 720 px file.
- Keep the deployment workflow and provenance checks. The site is also the proof of the second
  case study.
